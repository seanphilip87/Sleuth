MURDER AT THE MANOR
A Tangled Web of Lies

1. The Logic Puzzle

Every suspect has an alibi. Every suspect is lying. The innocent guests will eventually corroborate each other's secrets. The murderer's alibi is the only one that will clash. Look for physical clues in rooms to disprove lies early.

2. The Paranoia Meter

Deep probing raises high suspicion. Searching rooms and items raises minor suspicion. Keep an eye on the Paranoia tracker. If it reaches HIGH, the killer will start hunting you down to silence you.

3. The Execution

If you take the murder weapon, a hidden physical countdown begins. You must walk back to the Crime Scene room with the weapon to trigger the lockdown and accuse the killer.
