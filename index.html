THE MECHANICS
A Detective's Work

1. Geographical Friction

Two random locations are inaccessible. Find utility items (Boots, Lantern, Key) to enter them.

2. The Interrogation Web

Every suspect holds 5 facts. A polite Grunt reveals up to 2 before they get annoyed. A Deep Insult reveals up to 2 more, but spikes Hostility. Warning: Insulting the true Killer will massively spike their paranoia. Eating your Sandwich lowers Hostility.

3. The Grand Assembly

When you pocket the weapon, return to the Crime Scene. The innocents perfectly corroborate each other's alibis. The killer's alibi will be a lie. Accuse the killer and their true motive.
