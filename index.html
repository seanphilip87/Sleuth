MURDER AT THE MANOR
London, 1925. Lord Blackwood is Dead.

1. The Holo-Map

The screen is locked. Top is your map, middle is your log, bottom is your controls. Tap any adjacent tile on the map to walk there. The camera will follow you.

2. Trust No One

If you find a bloody item, do not touch it immediately. Search Rooms to find the hidden Forensic Magnifier to prove it isn't a red herring. If you take the true murder weapon, the killer will realize it. You will have 25 moves to survive.

3. Piss Them Off

Everyone is lying. When you question someone, Probe Deeper to push their buttons. Suspects have a hidden patience meter (1 to 3 probes). Some will crack and reveal scandalous affairs. Push past their limit? They will lawyer up and silence you.
